# Enabler Instructions

Use an Enabler when the primary outcome is technical, architectural, operational, compliance-related, exploratory or infrastructural.

Explain:

- what delivery or risk reduction it enables;
- why it is needed now;
- which Capability, Feature or User Story it supports;
- what evidence or outcome demonstrates completion;
- which constraints apply.

Do not disguise technical work as user value. State the technical purpose clearly, while still explaining its contribution to business delivery, risk reduction, compliance, learning or maintainability.

Keep the Enabler no larger than needed to enable the next meaningful delivery step.
